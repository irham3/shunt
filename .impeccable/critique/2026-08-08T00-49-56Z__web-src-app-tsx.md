## Priority Issues

### [P1] Narrow mobile widths clip Pay and Receipts

Why it matters: at 320px, public Pay panels remain 360px wide while root overflow is clipped. Important labels and revision details become unreachable. The 375px layout also overshoots slightly.

Fix: use a true single-column mobile rule or `minmax(min(100%, 360px), 1fr)`. Permit security headers to wrap. Test 320, 360, 375, 390, and 200% zoom.

Suggested command: `$impeccable adapt`

### [P1] Checkout reads like a contract simulator

Why it matters: a payer sees editable demo values and Execute Routing, but not a clear recipient identity, connected payer, fees, network consequence, permanence, or exit.

Fix: use a read-only invoice summary, identify payer and recipient, disclose asset, network, fees, and finality, rename the CTA to the actual amount, add Cancel, and move contract revision detail under a protection disclosure.

Suggested command: `$impeccable clarify`

### [P1] Policy deployment lacks review, validation, and undo

Why it matters: settings affect future income routing and locks. A malformed destination or long lock can be committed without a clear final comprehension step.

Fix: persist edits as a draft, validate addresses, add Reset, show before and after values plus a sample inflow, confirm lock or destination changes, and expose policy history.

Suggested command: `$impeccable harden`

### [P1] Feedback semantics can contradict or exclude users

Why it matters: error messages can show a lime success icon and status role. Receipt rows are click-only divs. Several fields lack strong labeling and focus treatment.

Fix: add semantic notification variants, use real buttons, associate labels, add live regions, restore focus styles, and raise low-contrast small labels.

Suggested command: `$impeccable audit`

### [P2] Naming and prototype utilities fragment the mental model

Why it matters: Set rules, Policy, Configure, Vault, Savings, Payment Receipts, Settlement History, and Audit Receipts compete. Test utilities sit next to real money tasks.

Fix: use Configure, Pay, and Receipts as the task spine. Keep Policy, revision, and Soroban as secondary technical detail. Move simulation and developer tools into a clearly marked test area.

Suggested command: `$impeccable distill`
